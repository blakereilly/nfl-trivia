How to Play FumbLe

1. Every day brings 2 rounds — Easy, then Hard. You'll see an NFL player's position and career stats (team, rookie year included); guess who it is.

2. You get 4 guesses per round. Stuck? Use "Get Hint" — it costs a guess, same as a wrong answer.

3. Hints arrive in order: Conference, then Division, then Team.

4. Your daily score is the total guesses/hints used across both rounds — lower is better. Missing a round costs 5.

5. Everyone gets the same 2 players each day. Share your score and keep your streak alive!

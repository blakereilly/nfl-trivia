How to Play

1. Every day has 2 rounds — Easy, then Hard — each showing one NFL player's position and career stats, including team and rookie year. Guess who it is.

2. You get 4 guesses per round. Stuck? Use a hint — it costs a guess, same as a wrong answer.

3. Hints arrive in order: Conference, then Division, then Team.

4. Your daily score is the total guesses/hints used across both rounds — lower is better.

5. Everyone plays the same 2 players each day. Share your score, keep a streak going, and see who knows ball.
